Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 12, 2026

TrueRank (chrisputer.tech) is operated by Chrisputer Labs. We collect as little as we can get away with. This page explains exactly what we store, why, and what your choices are.

What we collect

  • •Search queries. The questions you submit are stored and may be published as public research pages. Don't put personal information in a search query.
  • •Account data. If you create an account, we store your email address and a salted, one-way hash of your password (we never store the password itself), plus the list of searches you ran while signed in so you can revisit them.
  • •Email subscriptions. If you ask to be notified about a report, we store that email for that purpose only.
  • •Salted IP hashes. For rate limiting and click analytics we store a salted, truncated one-way hash of your IP address — never the raw IP. The secret salt is what makes the hash impractical to reverse back into an address.
  • •Chat messages. Messages you send to the "Talk about it" assistant are processed to generate a reply and are not stored by us afterwards.

What we don't collect

No advertising profiles built by us, no sale of personal data, no third-party trackers beyond the services listed below, no raw IP logs, no fingerprinting.

Cookies and local storage

  • •Theme preference is kept in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device.
  • •Session cookie. Signing in sets one HttpOnly, Secure cookie that keeps you signed in for 30 days. Signing out deletes it.
  • •Advertising cookies. See the advertising section below.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

We display ads served by Google AdSense. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings, or opt out of many third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info. Google's own privacy practices are described in the Google partner sites policy.

Affiliate links

As an Amazon Associate, Chrisputer Labs earns from qualifying purchases. Outbound buy links may carry an affiliate tag; retailers may set their own cookies when you click through. Affiliate relationships never influence our rankings. See our full disclosure on the About page.

Other services we rely on

  • •Cloudflare hosts the site and provides cookieless, privacy-first analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics).
  • •Google Search — for products Amazon doesn't sell, our /find link redirects you to a Google search for your query; that query is sent to Google as part of the redirect.
  • •AI providers. Research and chat are generated by large language models accessed through OpenRouter. Queries are processed to produce reports and replies.
  • •Search providers. We send the words you type into the search box to Serper.dev, Brave Search, DuckDuckGo, and Hacker News search (through Algolia). This helps us find review pages for your report.
  • •Page reader. To read the text of a review or product page, we send its web address to Jina Reader (r.jina.ai). If that service can't read a page, or the page is a video, we fetch it directly instead. Only the page address goes to these services, not personal data.
  • •Email notifications. If you ask for an email when a report is ready, we store your address in our database (Cloudflare D1) and send the message through Hostinger, our mailbox provider. Your address is used only for the notifications you requested. Every notification contains a one-click unsubscribe link.

Your choices and rights

You can use the entire research side of the site without an account. To delete your account, your search history, or a subscription email, contact us from the relevant email address and we'll remove it. Depending on where you live (e.g. EU/EEA under GDPR, California under CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you — the same contact address handles those requests.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be noted on this page.
