Case file 0417

The verdict, published like a finding

This is the moment TrueRank exists for: an overall score, a plain-language verdict, and every claim traced back to where it came from — and whether it survived contact with independent evidence.

Overall score

61 / 100

"Falls short of its headline claims."

Runtime and charge-speed figures hold up under independent testing. The "commercial-grade" durability claim and the 10-year warranty language do not match the terms buyers actually receive.

Sources checked: 38 Independent reviewers: 21 of 38 Sponsored content flagged: 6

"1,024Wh capacity, full recharge in 58 minutes"

Verified

Bench tests from two independent labs measured 1,011–1,038Wh and 54–61 minute recharge times, both within the claimed range.1,2

"Whisper-quiet operation, under 30dB"

Partially verified

True under light load (26–28dB). Under sustained high-draw charging, owner recordings and one lab test measured 34–39dB.3

"Commercial-grade, built for daily job-site use"

Unsubstantiated

No third-party durability or IP-rating certification found. Manufacturer cites no standard, no test body, and no published data.

"Full 10-year warranty, no questions asked"

Contradicted

The registered warranty document caps full coverage at 24 months, with a prorated 3–10 year period requiring proof of registration within 30 days.4

1. PowerBench Labs, independent teardown, 2026-05. 2. r/solargeneration community test thread (verified purchase, non-affiliate). 3. TrueRank field capture, 3 unaffiliated owners. 4. Manufacturer warranty PDF, archived 2026-06-01.

Method

How a verdict gets built

1

Extract the claims

We pull every checkable claim from the listing, the manual, and the marketing copy — specs, warranty terms, certifications, comparative statements.

2

Weigh the sources

Independent labs, unaffiliated owners, and long-standing forum threads are weighted up. Sponsored posts, affiliate listicles, and marketplace stars are weighted down or excluded.

3

Publish the finding

Each claim gets a verdict and a citation trail. The overall score is the average, weighted by how material each claim is to your decision.

Editorial standards

Independence is the whole product

We earn a commission on some purchases made through this site. That commission never touches a verdict, a score, or a claim status. If we get that wrong, the product stops meaning anything — so here is exactly how we keep the line.

  • Reviewer independence is checked, not assumed. We flag sponsored posts, free-unit reviews, and affiliate-only listicles before they ever influence a score.

  • No vendor buys placement. Nothing ranks higher because of a commercial relationship.

  • Every citation is retrievable. Click any footnote and see the original source, not a paraphrase.

Products get re-audited as warranties, specs, and reviews evolve. We'll email you if something you follow moves.
