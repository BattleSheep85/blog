How a verdict gets made

Three steps, all automated, all shown to you afterward.

1

Extract the claims

We pull every testable claim from the product's own listing, packaging copy, and ads — not what other people say about it.

2

Search independent evidence

Lab tests, expert reviews, teardown reports, and verified owner experiences — weighted by source credibility, not popularity.

3

Score each claim honestly

Verified, partially verified, unsubstantiated, or contradicted — with the sources shown, so you can disagree with us.

Why trust the verdict

We make money differently than most review sites, and we think you should know how.

We don't get paid to say yes

Some links on TrueRank are affiliate links — if you buy through them, we may earn a small commission. That commission is identical whether the verdict is "verified" or "contradicted." It never affects scoring.

Every source is shown

No verdict ships without citations you can click through and check yourself. If we're wrong, you can see exactly why we were wrong.

Reviews are screened, not just counted

We flag incentivized, sponsored, and bot-pattern reviews before they factor into anything — a 4.8★ average means little if a third of it is fake.

Verdicts get re-checked

Products change, firmware updates ship, prices move. We re-run verification and notify you if a verdict on something you checked changes.
