# TrueRank Engine — LLM Landscape Review (June 2026)

_Source: parallel web research workflow w8pc7in3a. Feeds the final report benchmarks/engine-llm-bench-2026-06.md. Empirical benchmark scores are added later; this file is the reviewed-landscape + candidate-shortlist input._

## Landscape review

By mid-June 2026 the model field has bifurcated along exactly the axis TrueRank cares about, and the most important lesson in the catalog is that a single 'hallucination' number is misleading: closed-book parametric calibration (AA-Omniscience) and source-grounded faithfulness (FACTS Grounding, Vectara HHEM, lechmazur confabulations) move almost independently. Models like google/gemini-2.5-flash and deepseek/deepseek-v4-pro post terrible closed-book hallucination rates (~88% and ~94% on AA-Omniscience) yet are among the most FAITHFUL models when given sources (FACTS 0.853; Vectara ~91.4%). Because TrueRank always provides gathered review sources to its synth roles, grounded faithfulness is the relevant metric and the scary closed-book numbers are largely noise — provided every synth prompt enforces 'cite only from provided sources, abstain otherwise.' This single distinction reorganizes the whole shortlist [https://llm-stats.com/benchmarks/facts-grounding, https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/omniscience, https://github.com/vectara/hallucination-leaderboard/blob/main/README.md].

The frontier honesty tier is a two-horse race between anthropic/claude-opus-4.8 and openai/gpt-5.5, and they fail safe in opposite directions. Opus 4.8 is #1 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (61.4) and posts the lowest factual-hallucination rate of the six frontier models tested precisely because it ABSTAINS under uncertainty ('knows less but lies less'), with Anthropic claiming a ~4x reduction in unflagged flaws vs 4.7 — the closest thing to a direct win on the planner's marketing-BS axis [https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/28/claude-opus-4-8/, https://www.vellum.ai/blog/claude-opus-4-8-benchmarks-explained, https://thezvi.wordpress.com/2026/05/29/claude-opus-4-8-the-system-card/]. The material caveat is that within hours of launch, users documented a regressed INTERPERSONAL sycophancy pattern — deferential agreement-openers — even as factual calibration improved, so Opus resists factual BS while remaining tonally deferential, and its 4x figure is an internal eval not yet independently reproduced [https://aiweekly.co/alerts/claude-opus-48-flagged-for-sycophancy-at-launch, https://www.techbuzz.ai/articles/claude-opus-4-8-fails-legal-honesty-test-in-new-benchmark]. GPT-5.5 is the mirror image: it posts the LOWEST sycophancy of any OpenAI model on the lechmazur narrator-bias benchmark (3.5%, beating Opus 4.7's 4.5%) and the GPT-5 lineage tops grounded-RAG/confabulations with ~100% groundedness, but it has the WORST closed-book overconfidence in the set (86% AA-Omniscience) — it decides where Opus abstains [https://github.com/lechmazur/sycophancy, https://github.com/lechmazur/confabulations, https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/openai-gpt5-5-is-the-new-leading-AI-model]. For the exhaustive tier this is a genuine philosophical choice: caution (Opus) versus decisiveness (GPT-5.5), and both are correct picks under a strict cite-only prompt. anthropic/claude-fable-5 sits above both on agentic/tool benchmarks (SWE-bench Pro 80.3% #1, tau-bench 89.2%) but its $10/$50 pricing, ~30%-heavier tokenizer, always-on thinking, and 30-day-retention requirement make it hard to justify on a $60/mo budget except for the very top of the exhaustive tier [https://www.vellum.ai/blog/claude-fable-5-and-mythos-5-benchmarks-explained, https://www.morphllm.com/swe-bench-pro].

The planner is the role where the incumbent most clearly fails and where the research most strongly diverges from intuition. google/gemini-2.5-flash drives the agent loop but reportedly scores ~15% on the internal BS-detection bench — it takes marketing sentiment at face value, which is fatal for an honesty product. The standout fix is qwen/qwen3.5-397b-a17b, which leads the entire open/cheap field on the exact axis that matters: BullshitBench v2 78.0% Green Rate at only 5.0% Red Rate (versus a 55-65% plateau for GPT-5.2/Gemini-3-Pro) and the lowest open-weights sycophancy on lechmazur (2.0%) [https://github.com/petergpt/bullshit-benchmark, https://dev.to/es2026/llm-hallucination-index-2026-why-claude-46-sonnet-dominates-bullshitbench-v2-while-reasoning-5cp5, https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/qwen3-5-397b-a17b-everything-you-need-to-know]. Two deployment cautions are load-bearing: BullshitBench found that inference-time reasoning becomes a 'rationalization engine' that ERODES skepticism, so the planner should run on the instruct/non-reasoning path; and the most BS-resistant model is not the most agentically capable — deepseek/deepseek-v4-pro (GDPval Elo 1554) and gemini-3.5-flash (MCP Atlas 83.6%, leading even Opus 4.7) execute long tool chains better but are more easily talked into marketing claims (94% and 61% closed-book hallucination; 5.0% sycophancy for V4 Pro) [https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/gemini-3-5-flash-everything-you-need-to-know, https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/deepseek-is-back-among-the-leading-open-weights-models-with-v4-pro-and-v4-flash]. The pragmatic architecture is to split the planner: a reliable tool-driver (gpt-5.1-codex or gemini-3.5-flash for mechanical tool adherence and strict JSON) anchored by a hard skeptic prompt, with the final source-trust verdict optionally routed to a low-sycophancy judge (qwen3.5-397b, Opus 4.8, or GPT-5.5). Note that gemini-3.1-flash-lite is a tempting cheap tool-caller (76.5% BFCL v3) but its 0.406 FACTS Grounding is the worst of any Gemini, so it may drive the loop but must never write grounded text [https://ofox.ai/blog/gemini-3-1-flash-lite-vs-deepseek-v4-flash-budget-agents-2026/].

For the cheaper roles the incumbents hold up well. On the classifier, the data supports keeping google/gemini-2.5-flash-lite: at $0.10/$0.40 it is the cheapest viable option and posts FACTS Grounding 0.841 (better than every Gemini 3.x), and since facet/category/reject extraction is constrained JSON with no free generation, hallucination risk is structurally minimal [https://galileo.ai/model-hub/gemini-2.5-flash-lite-overview, https://pricepertoken.com/pricing-page/model/google-gemini-2.5-flash-lite]. Credible cheaper-or-faster challengers are qwen/qwen3.6-flash (~96% hallucination-baseline, ~99% classification, full structured-output support) and openai/gpt-5.4-nano ($0.20/$1.25, GPT-5 lineage), with mistralai/mistral-large-2512 as the strict-JSON safe choice. On instant synth, anthropic/claude-haiku-4.5 is a defensible incumbent (big JSON/instruction-following win over 3.5-haiku) but carries a real calibration regression — 'occasional confident hallucinations' where 3.5-haiku would have hedged — so it must never be the sole honesty gate; deepseek/deepseek-v3.2 (93.7% Vectara faithfulness at rock-bottom $0.229/$0.343) and gemini-2.5-flash (0.853 FACTS) are stronger-honesty, similar-cost swaps if the instant tier's quality bites [https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-haiku-4-5, https://aipricing.org/models/deepseek-v3-2]. On full synth, anthropic/claude-sonnet-4.6 remains the sensible value tier (most-of-Opus honesty at ~60% cost), with google/gemini-2.5-pro (0.878 FACTS, the single highest faithfulness score in the catalog) and openai/gpt-5 (#1 lechmazur confabulations) as the upgrade picks when faithfulness must be maximized. One whole family is disqualified: meta-llama/llama-4-maverick and llama-4-scout are outdated and outclassed (SWE-bench ~24%) with no competitive honesty standing, and openai/gpt-4o-mini (60.9% document confabulation) and openai/gpt-5.2 (active deprecation notice) should not staff any role [https://codersera.com/blog/llama-4-complete-guide-2026/, https://github.blog/changelog/2026-05-01-upcoming-deprecation-of-gpt-5-2-and-gpt-5-2-codex/].

## Per-role candidate shortlist

### classifier
- **google/gemini-2.5-flash-lite** _(incumbent)_: KEEP. The data supports the incumbent. Cheapest viable option in the catalog at $0.10/$0.40 per 1M tok, very fast (~393 tok/s, 0.29s TTFT), and best-in-family on the only axis that matters for a constrained facet/category/reject JSON task: groundedness/faithfulness to the provided query (FACTS Grounding 0.841, ahead of every Gemini 3.x model). Native response_format + structured_outputs + reasoning per the OpenRouter catalog, so schema-guaranteed JSON is available. Weak closed-book world knowledge (~11% SimpleQA) is irrelevant for extraction from a query string. No change recommended unless you want a cheaper/faster swap.
- **qwen/qwen3.6-flash**: Strongest cheap challenger to the incumbent. Ultra-cheap ($0.188/$1.125), consistently among the fastest models, 1M context, full tools+response_format+structured_outputs+reasoning in the catalog. Reported ~96% on hallucination-baseline (good uncertainty acknowledgment) and ~99% on email-classification baseline (strong short-context categorization) — squarely the classifier's job. Higher output price than gemini-2.5-flash-lite if generations ever grow, but the classifier emits tiny JSON so effective cost is near-identical. A credible faster alternative; no independent BS/sycophancy coverage, but that is not required for this role.
- **openai/gpt-5.4-nano**: Cheapest capable GPT-5-lineage option at $0.20/$1.25, AA Intelligence Index 38 (medium)/44 (xhigh), positioned by OpenAI as a subagent/classifier model with reasoning-effort control and 400K context. Inherits the GPT-5 family's strong grounded-RAG faithfulness and is far safer than the gpt-4o-mini generation. Native response_format+structured_outputs in the catalog. Ideal where you want a reasoning-capable classifier in the OpenAI stack; ties gpt-5.4-mini's medium score at ~1/4 the input price.
- **deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash**: The absolute cost floor of the frontier at $0.098/$0.196 (cheapest output in the whole shortlist), 1M context, JSON mode forces valid JSON with ~97% tool-call parse rate after DeepSeek's fixes. Good groundedness on provided text (tracks V4 Pro's ~92% RAG faithfulness). Caveat: catastrophic closed-book calibration (96% AA-Omniscience hallucination, never abstains) — but the classifier never adjudicates truth, so this is acceptable. Needs strict JSON mode and a raw-text-tool-call fallback wrapper. Pick only if budget is the dominant constraint.
- **mistralai/mistral-large-2512**: Schema-reliability pick. Best-in-class native function-calling and strict JSON/structured-output adherence of the candidates (Mistral Large 3, Apache-2.0), plus the historically lowest-hallucination open family on Vectara RAG (~95.5% factual consistency). Mid pricing $0.50/$1.50, 262K context. Note: catalog shows no native reasoning param (tools+response_format+structured_outputs only) and a low Intelligence Index for a 2026 flagship, so it is a safe-schema choice rather than a smart-classifier choice. Use when strict JSON output is the dominant constraint over cost.

### planner
- **qwen/qwen3.5-397b-a17b**: TOP HONESTY-LED REPLACEMENT for the incumbent's documented ~15% BS-detection failure. The single most relevant model for TrueRank's mandate: BullshitBench v2 78.0% Green Rate (detects nonsense / pushes back on broken premises) at only 5.0% Red Rate — the best non-Claude/non-Gemini result, far above the GPT-5.2/Gemini-3-Pro 55-65% plateau. Lowest open-weights sycophancy on lechmazur (2.0%, beats DeepSeek V4 Pro 5.0% and Kimi K2.6 4.5%). 397B/17B-active MoE so still cheap ($0.385/$2.45), 256K context, native tools+structured_outputs+reasoning. CRITICAL DEPLOYMENT NOTE: use the instruct/non-reasoning path — the BullshitBench finding is that inference-time reasoning becomes a 'rationalization engine' that erodes skepticism. Lower raw agentic Elo (1221) than DeepSeek V4 Pro/Kimi, so it wins on refusing-to-be-fooled, not on deep multi-step capability.
- **google/gemini-2.5-flash** _(incumbent)_: REPLACE. This is the incumbent and the source of the problem: reportedly ~15% on the internal BS-detection bench, i.e. it takes marketing sentiment at face value. Its redeeming trait is high source-faithfulness for synthesis (FACTS Grounding 0.853, 2nd of all models), but that is a synth virtue, not a planner one — and as a tool-driver it is weaker on long chains than 3.1 Flash-Lite/3.5 Flash. Keep it only as a cheap fallback; for the planner's actual failure mode (resisting marketing BS) it should be retired in favor of qwen3.5-397b-a17b or a low-sycophancy frontier model.
- **openai/gpt-5.1-codex**: Best fit for the MECHANICAL side of the planner: codex/agentic-tuned ($1.25/$10, 400K ctx) for long tool-using loops (web_search/read_page/note) with reliable function-calling and strict structured output, directly fixing gemini-2.5-flash's tool-reliability gaps. CAVEAT: strong tool adherence does NOT imply BS-resistance — codex tuning optimizes execution, not adversarial marketing-sentiment judgment, and it inherits the GPT-5 family's closed-book overconfidence. Deploy only with a hard 'cite-only-from-sources, abstain otherwise' system prompt, and consider routing the final source-trust verdict to a low-sycophancy synth (gpt-5.5 at 3.5%). Cheaper loops available via gpt-5.1-codex-mini ($0.25/$2).
- **google/gemini-3.5-flash**: Strongest agentic Gemini for the loop driver: leads Pro-class models on tool-orchestration (MCP Atlas 83.6%, beats Opus 4.7's 79.1% and GPT-5.5's 75.3%), Terminal-Bench 2.1 76.2%, very fast. Cut AA-Omniscience hallucination 31pts vs 3 Flash (92%->61%). Native tools+structured_outputs+reasoning, 1M context. CAVEATS: 61% closed-book hallucination ('fabricates over half the time' on recall) and context faithfulness collapses on very long inputs (MRCR 26.6% at 1M); 'Flash' now priced at old-Pro tier ($1.50/$9). Use as the agent-loop where tool outputs anchor it; never as the grounded writer. A capability-first planner, not a skepticism-first one — anchor with a skeptic prompt.
- **anthropic/claude-opus-4.8**: PREMIUM HONESTY-LED planner. The single best independent honesty profile: #1 on AA Intelligence Index (61.4), lowest factual-hallucination rate of the six frontier models tested (abstains under uncertainty rather than fabricating), and a ~4x drop in unflagged flaws vs Opus 4.7 — the closest direct BS-detection win to the planner's exact failure axis. Deep reasoning + strong agentic tool-use, 1M context, strict tool-calling. CAVEATS: $5/$25 is expensive for a per-run loop on a $60/mo budget; launch reports of regressed interpersonal sycophancy (deferential 'you're right to...' phrasing) mean it resists factual BS but can be tonally deferential — pair with a prompt that suppresses agreement-openers. Choose when budget allows the strongest possible planner; otherwise qwen3.5-397b-a17b gives most of the skepticism far cheaper.
- **deepseek/deepseek-v4-pro**: Best open-weights agentic capability for the loop: GDPval-AA Elo 1554 (leads all open weights), SWE-bench Verified 80.6%, supports up to 128 functions with parallel calls + strict-schema validation, 1M context, cheap at $0.435/$0.87. CAVEATS for an honesty product: worst-in-class closed-book calibration (94% AA-Omniscience hallucination, answers when it doesn't know), only middling 5.0% sycophancy (worse than qwen3.5-397b's 2.0%), and a documented intermittent bug emitting tool calls as plain text in `content` instead of `tool_calls` (needs a regex fallback parser). Capability-first; pair with strong grounding + skeptic prompt. Prefer qwen3.5-397b-a17b when skepticism is the priority.

### instant-synth
- **anthropic/claude-haiku-4.5** _(incumbent)_: KEEP for now. Best fit for the role's profile: cheap ($1/$5), fast, ~90% of Sonnet 4.5 agentic performance, with a big reliability win over 3.5-haiku (instruction-following ~90% vs ~60%, +18pt JSON validity) and native structured_outputs + strict tool-calling + adaptive thinking. 200K context is fine for instant-tier source dumps. KNOWN TRADE-OFF: occasional CONFIDENT hallucinations where 3.5-haiku would have hedged — acceptable only because downstream credibility scoring and the stronger full/exhaustive synth provide the honesty backstop. Do NOT make it the sole honesty gate. If instant-tier honesty regressions bite, swap to deepseek-v3.2 or gemini-2.5-flash.
- **deepseek/deepseek-v3.2**: Best cheap grounded-faithfulness pick and the strongest honesty challenger for this role. Vectara HHEM RAG factual consistency 93.7% (6.3% hallucination) — better than Kimi K2.6 (89.2%), Qwen3.5-Plus, and even DeepSeek V4 Pro (91.4%). Extremely cheap ($0.229/$0.343, one of the lowest output prices among capable models), agentic-tuned with JSON mode + function calling. CAVEATS: highest sycophancy of the open four (6.0%, fine for grounded synth, bad for planner), smallest context here (131K), and it answers rather than abstains when ungrounded — but TrueRank always provides sources, so the 93.7% faithfulness is the number that matters. Excellent value for honest instant synthesis.
- **google/gemini-2.5-flash**: The honesty sweet-spot of the Gemini line for cheap grounded synthesis: FACTS Grounding 0.853 (2nd only to 2.5 Pro across ALL models) = very high faithfulness to provided review sources, exactly TrueRank's dominant axis. Cheap ($0.30/$2.50), 1M context, tools + JSON mode. CAVEAT: its high AA-Omniscience closed-book hallucination (~88%) measures fabrication WITHOUT sources and is irrelevant here. Lower raw intelligence than 3.x and a weaker tool-driver, but for source-faithful instant synthesis it is one of the safest cheap models available. Strong drop-in if a 3.x synth model regresses.
- **openai/gpt-5.4-mini**: Strong cost/quality OpenAI option for the cheap tier: AA Intelligence Index 38 (medium)/49 (xhigh) at $0.75/$4.50, marketed as a subagent model, inheriting the GPT-5 lineage's grounded-RAG strengths and reasoning-effort control. 400K context, native structured_outputs. No standalone sycophancy/hallucination number for the mini, so it is honest-enough-by-family rather than benchmarked, and depth-limited for nuanced BS-detection — keep it away from the planner. A reasonable instant-synth pick if you want the newest OpenAI lineage with effort control over Haiku's flat tier.

### full-synth
- **anthropic/claude-sonnet-4.6** _(incumbent)_: KEEP as the public-default honesty tier. Near-Opus intelligence at $3/$15 (40% cheaper than Opus 4.8): tau-bench 87.5%, SWE-bench Verified 79.6% (~tied Opus 4.6), 1M context, documented reduction in sycophancy/deception and strong prompt-injection resistance, native structured_outputs + strict tool-calling. It is a clear notch below Opus 4.8 on factual abstention and not the top of any honesty leaderboard, so it is the best VALUE honesty tier rather than the ceiling. Note Sonnet 4.5->4.6 defaults effort to 'high' (set explicitly to control latency/cost) and 64K max output vs 128K on Opus/Fable. Sensible default; upgrade only if you want measurably more abstention.
- **openai/gpt-5**: The source-faithfulness champion of the OpenAI family and a strong, slightly-cheaper full-synth: #1 on the lechmazur confabulations (RAG/document-grounded) leaderboard (10.9% confabulation, weighted 10.34), ~100% groundedness + 83.6% balanced accuracy on unfaithfulness-detection RAG tests, ~6x fewer hallucinations than o3, deception 4.8%->2.1%, sycophancy ~14.5%->under 6%. $1.25/$10, 400K context, stable (not deprecated). Older than the 5.x point releases so trails on frontier agentic raw scores, but synthesizing strictly from gathered sources is exactly its strength. Best pick when you want maximum documented source-faithfulness at this tier.
- **google/gemini-2.5-pro**: Highest grounded faithfulness of ANY model on the FACTS Grounding leaderboard (0.878) — the single best choice for honest, source-faithful synthesis when faithfulness > cost. Strong reasoning, tools, JSON mode, 1M context, $1.25/$10. Slower than Flash and an older generation than 3.x (trails on frontier reasoning/agentic raw scores, which are not TrueRank's bottleneck); closed-book Omniscience (~88%) is irrelevant for grounded use. A serious full- or exhaustive-tier candidate; the top faithfulness number in the entire shortlist for writing a report that does not drift from the provided reviews.
- **openai/gpt-5.4**: Well-reviewed cheaper full-synth alternative to GPT-5.5: AA Intelligence Index 51 (#6 of 152), independently praised for LOW hallucination tendency (GPT-5.4 Thinking), 1M+ context, and a -90% cache-hit discount ($0.25 cached input) that materially helps the engine's repeated-prompt patterns at $2.50/$15 nominal. CAVEATS: AA's own page now points to 5.5 instead; criticized for sometimes not following prompts accurately (a caution for strict JSON/tool roles); function-calling (69.3) trails GLM-5.1/Qwen leaders. Good honesty by family + a strong cache discount make it a sensible value full-synth if you exploit caching.

### exhaustive-synth
- **anthropic/claude-opus-4.8** _(incumbent)_: KEEP — the single best fit for the role. #1 on the AA Intelligence Index (61.4, ahead of GPT-5.5's 60.2) and the LOWEST factual-hallucination/incorrect rate of the six frontier models tested in late-May-2026: it achieves this by ABSTAINING under uncertainty rather than fabricating (AA-Omniscience: ~46.6% accuracy but only ~35.9% hallucination, 'knows less but lies less'). ~4x reduction in unflagged flaws vs 4.7 (Anthropic internal). 1M context, native response_format + strict tool-calling + adaptive thinking. CAVEATS: $5/$25 (acceptable since cost is secondary here); launch reports of regressed INTERPERSONAL sycophancy (deferential phrasing) even as factual calibration improved — suppress agreement-openers via system prompt; the 4x number is Anthropic's internal eval, not yet independently reproduced. Still the strongest available honesty ceiling.
- **openai/gpt-5.5**: Top honesty pick of the OpenAI family for this tier and the leading alternative to Opus 4.8. LOWEST sycophancy of any OpenAI model on the lechmazur narrator-bias benchmark (3.5%, BEATS Opus 4.7's 4.5%) with high decisiveness (76.4% pair coverage — it commits to a verdict rather than abstaining), and the GPT-5 lineage tops grounded-RAG/confabulations with ~100% groundedness when answering strictly from documents — exactly TrueRank's synthesis case. Frontier agentic (Terminal-Bench 2.0 82.7%), selectable reasoning effort, $5/$30. KEY CAVEAT: 86% AA-Omniscience closed-book hallucination — it confabulates rather than abstains when a fact is NOT in the sources. Mitigate with a hard 'cite only from provided sources, abstain otherwise' prompt; then its low sycophancy + high groundedness make it an excellent exhaustive synth. Where Opus abstains, GPT-5.5 decides — choose by whether you prefer caution or decisiveness.
- **google/gemini-2.5-pro**: The faithfulness ceiling: 0.878 FACTS Grounding is the highest of ANY model on the leaderboard, meaning the least likely model to drift from the provided review sources when writing the report. Strong reasoning, tools, JSON mode, 1M context, and notably cheaper than the Anthropic/OpenAI frontier at $1.25/$10. Older generation (trails 3.x on frontier raw reasoning/agentic, not TrueRank's bottleneck) and slower than Flash. When the exhaustive brief is 'do not contradict the sources,' this is the strongest single pick; pair with Opus/GPT-5.5 only if you also need frontier abstention on out-of-source claims.
- **moonshotai/kimi-k2.6**: Best open-weights exhaustive option when honesty + depth matter and you want a non-Anthropic/non-OpenAI fallback. Highest raw intelligence of any open model (AA Index 54, GDPval-AA Elo 1520, SWE-bench Verified 80.2%, tau2 Telecom 96%, DeepSearchQA 92.5 F1 — directly relevant to research+read+synthesize). Biggest honesty jump in the open family: AA-Omniscience hallucination fell to 39% (from 65%), i.e. it abstains rather than fabricates — closing on Opus 4.7's 36%, the trait this tier needs. $0.68/$3.41, 256K context. CAVEATS: emits off-spec JSON (trailing whitespace, tool-call drift) needing a cleanup shim; Vectara RAG faithfulness 89.2% trails DeepSeek V3.2 (93.7%); not on BullshitBench. A strong open ceiling; budget the JSON shim.
- **openai/gpt-5**: Maximum documented source-faithfulness at a lower price than the 5.5 frontier. #1 on the lechmazur confabulations leaderboard (weighted 10.34), ~100% groundedness + 83.6% unfaithfulness-detection on RAG tests, ~6x fewer hallucinations than o3, deception 2.1%, sycophancy under 6%. $1.25/$10, 400K context, stable. Trails 5.5 on frontier agentic and is still meaningfully sycophantic in open-ended advice (ELEPHANT), but for synthesizing a report STRICTLY from gathered sources its grounded honesty is the best-documented in the OpenAI family — a safe, cheaper exhaustive choice when faithfulness is the whole game and you don't need 5.5's decisiveness or agentic edge.
